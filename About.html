From a practice in Vapi in 1969 to a multidisciplinary architectural firm, our journey has always been driven by design integrity, technical rigor, and long-term relationships.

WHERE IT BEGAN

Founded by Ar. Kirit B. Bhatt, Architects' Nook began its practice in Vapi in 1969 after he completed his Bachelor of Architecture from Sir J. J. School of Architecture, Mumbai.

What began as a modest practice gradually evolved into a multidisciplinary architectural firm with a growing portfolio of landmark buildings across South Gujarat and beyond.

Since 2007, the practice has evolved into a full-fledged corporate entity, carrying forward the same discipline, curiosity and commitment that shaped its earliest years.

THE PEOPLE

Founder & Principal Architect

Ar. Kirit B. Bhatt

Bachelor of Architecture, Sir J. J. School of Architecture, Mumbai — 1968

Started practice in Vapi in 1969. He served as Chairman of the Indian Institute of Architects, Gujarat Chapter for two consecutive terms and represented Dadra & Nagar Haveli in the Council of Architecture, New Delhi. He also served on the Executive Committee of the Indian Institute of Valuers, South Gujarat Branch, alongside various social organisations.

Project Director & Structural Engineer

Er. Manish Bhatt

Pune University — 1992

Joined the practice in 1994. Responsible for the engineering division, general administration, project management and valuation across major industrial, commercial, and institutional projects.

Principal Architect

Ar. Kashyap Bhatt

Pune University — 1992

Joined the practice in 2000. Design lead for master planning, architectural detailing, spatial choreography, and in-house 3D visualisation across diverse building typologies.
